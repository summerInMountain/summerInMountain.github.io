Privacy Policy

money-engine · Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. Who this applies to

money-engine is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. The only Google account that signs in to it is the owner's own. It has no other users.

2. Google user data we access

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), money-engine requests these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload approved videos to the owner's channels;
  • youtube.force-ssl: to manage those videos (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists, schedule) and to post or manage comments on them;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read view and watch-time statistics of the owner's own channels.

3. How we use it

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how those videos perform. Channel statistics (for example views or watch time per video) may be summarised and used to plan future video topics, which can include sending those summarised numbers to AI services the owner uses (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini). No personal data about viewers is collected.

4. Storage and security

The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted in a database on the owner's own computer. Channel statistics are stored in the same database. Nothing is stored on a public server.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for advertising. It is not used to develop, improve or train general AI or machine-learning models. It is not transferred to anyone except as described in section 3, or where the law requires it.

money-engine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops all uploads. The owner can delete the stored token and statistics by deleting the engine's database.
