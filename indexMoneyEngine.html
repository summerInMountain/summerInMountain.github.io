money-engine

A private publishing tool for one YouTube creator.

What it does

money-engine is a personal tool used only by its owner to run their own educational YouTube channels (Ke Mana Uangnya?, Itung Cepat and The Math They Skip). These channels explain the everyday math behind bills, fees and prices.

When the owner signs in with Google, money-engine uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails that the owner has approved to the owner's own channels;
  • set titles, descriptions, playlists and the publish schedule of those videos;
  • post and manage comments on the owner's own videos;
  • read view and watch-time statistics of the owner's own channels, to see which videos work.

money-engine is not offered to the public. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it never acts on other people's channels.
